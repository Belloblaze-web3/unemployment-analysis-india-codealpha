## LinkedIn post

As part of my **CodeAlpha Data Science Internship — Task 2**, I completed an end-to-end **Unemployment Analysis with Python** using monthly unemployment data from India.

The project covered:

- Data cleaning and validation with Pandas
- Monthly unemployment trend analysis
- Regional and rural/urban comparisons
- COVID-19 shock analysis
- Labour participation versus unemployment exploration
- Matplotlib visual storytelling
- Reproducible notebook and documentation

### Key findings

- The average unemployment rate increased from **9.51% before COVID-19** to **17.77% during the March–June 2020 shock window** — an **86.9% increase**.
- The national monthly average peaked at **24.88% in May 2020**.
- Rural unemployment increased by approximately **99.9%**, while urban unemployment increased by **77.8%** relative to their pre-COVID averages.
- Regional differences were substantial, with **Tripura** recording the highest average unemployment rate in this dataset.

The results reinforce an important analytical lesson: national averages are useful, but regional, rural/urban, and labour-participation views are essential for understanding labour-market stress and designing targeted policy responses.

The project includes a cleaned dataset, analysis notebook, visualizations, an executive report, and a reproducible Python pipeline.
