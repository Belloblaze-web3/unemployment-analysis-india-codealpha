COVID effects were uneven across regions

Geographic Variance

  • Extreme Shock: Puducherry and Tamil Nadu saw average rate increases of 20-35 percentage points, reflecting direct exposure to lockdown intensity.
  • Resilience: Jammu & Kashmir and Tripura actually saw slight decreases in their average rates during this specific window, suggesting different local economic drivers or data reporting lags.
  • Broad Stress: The majority of regions (24 of 28) saw an absolute increase in average unemployment.

This uneven impact confirms that tailored regional responses are more efficient than uniform national policies.

Source: India Unemployment Dataset (May 2019 – June 2020)
