Key observation

National unemployment is a composition of vastly different regional outcomes. The top five regions averaged 18.5% to 28.4%, while regions like Meghalaya (4.8%) and Odisha (5.6%) maintained significantly lower averages throughout the period.

Extreme Outlier:

Puducherry recorded a 76.7% peak during the COVID shock, illustrating how specific territories experienced complete labour-market shutdowns.
